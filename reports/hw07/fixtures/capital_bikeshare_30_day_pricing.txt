Capital Bikeshare 30-Day Membership. The 30-Day Membership costs $25. Members receive unlimited classic bike rides with the first 45 minutes included. Trips longer than 45 minutes cost $0.05 per additional minute. Members pay no bike unlock fee. A single classic-bike ride costs $1 to unlock plus $0.15 per minute.
